About Communise

Communise is a digital community noticeboard that helps people discover local businesses, services, events, and activities. Inspired by the noticeboards often found in supermarkets and community spaces, Communise brings local information together in one convenient place, making it easier to connect with and support local communities.

Purpose

Communise exists to strengthen local connections. It provides businesses, entrepreneurs, service providers, and community organisations with a simple platform to promote what they offer, while helping residents discover opportunities, services, and activities in their area.

Why Communise

The Problem Communise Solves

Communise provides a central place for local advertising and community information, eliminating the need to search through weeks or months of Facebook posts, WhatsApp messages, and social media feeds to find a service, event, recommendation, or opportunity that was previously shared.

By bringing local listings together in one searchable location, Communise makes local information easier to find, helping people discover and support the businesses, services, and community activities that matter most to them.
